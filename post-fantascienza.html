Science fiction: a singular genre between spectacle and concept

Sci-FiFantascienzaGenereKubrick

ABSTRACT

Science fiction occupies a singular position among film genres: halfway between the spectacular blockbuster and the film of ideas. This article analyses the internal tension within the genre between the visual and conceptual dimensions, through examples ranging from Kubrick to Villeneuve.
