Shot 1 Medium shot / Close-up — JIM

Jim sits at a table with his forearms resting on it, facing the room with a smiling, reassuring look — waiting to welcome the woman he is expecting. The setting is a refined, silent and elegant lounge bar in Art Déco style, with dark green velvet sofas, walnut chairs and abstract paintings in gold and green. The venue is empty, bathed in warm amber lighting.

Shot 2 Medium long shot / Wide — BARBARA

From Jim's perspective we see the entrance door across the room opening: Barbara enters, slightly nervous, scanning the venue with an investigative gaze. In the background, the wall behind the bar with many bottles on display, in a warm, slightly dimmed atmosphere punctuated by intense wall sconces.

Shot 3 Medium long shot / Wide — JIM

Semi-wide shot of the room with several empty tables. Jim stands and walks forward — from his table across the venue towards Barbara (off-camera), making a welcoming gesture with his hand.

Shot 4 Medium shot — JIM, BARBARA and the waiter

Jim seated with his back to camera, looking at Barbara. She sits opposite him at the same table, returning his gaze. On the right, a waiter in his 50s or 60s discreetly approaches to place a glass of red wine on the table — ordered by Jim for Barbara. The bar wall is visible in the background.

Jim and Barbara sit facing each other, forearms resting on the table. They stare into each other's eyes in a moment of silent, intense connection. She places her hands over his. On the table: Jim's whisky glass, nearly empty, and Barbara's wine glass almost drained. The bar wall is visible but blurred in the background.

Shot 10 Two-shot / Tight close-up

Tight framing of the two protagonists at the table. Jim gently takes Barbara's head and neck to draw her closer, while she places her hand on his chest with tender sensuality. Their lips brush at the very edge of a kiss. He is wearing a wedding ring — though that's a detail of little importance…
