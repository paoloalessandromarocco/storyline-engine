AELITA — or The Decline of Mars

Graphic Novel adaptation based on the novel by Aleksey Nikolayevich Tolstoy (1922).

Panel 01 Scene I

Engineer Mstislav Los posts a recruitment notice for a journey to Mars through the desolate streets of Petrograd following the October Revolution.

Panel 02 Scene II

Aleksei Gusev, a Red Army soldier and pragmatic, dynamic Bolshevik, joins the expedition. Their first meeting takes place in Los's workshop.

Panel 03 Scene III

Launch of the metallic rocket amidst smoke and atomic thrusts before a curious crowd, hopeful to spread revolutionary thought into outer space.

Panel 04 Scene IV

Daily life inside the spherical cabin during deep space navigation. Los shows cosmic maps to his traveling companion.

Panel 05 Scene V

The two Earthlings take their first steps on Martian soil amid red rocks, cacti, and alien flora. No sign of life, let alone civilization.

Panel 06 Scene VI

Undeterred by their ideals, after a few kilometers they come across the ruins of what appears to be an abandoned Martian city.

Panel 07 Scene VII

Continuing their trek, they realize civilization is still intact. The Earthlings offer a message of peace, but are surrounded by soldiers with blue-green skin.

Panel 08 Scene VIII

Escorted to the capital, they meet Tuskub, the ruler of Mars. No real dialogue takes place with the inscrutable dictator.

Panel 09 Scene IX

A private meeting in the princely residence of Aelita, Tuskub's daughter. The young woman shows Los the historical attempts to rebuild Martian civilization.

Panel 10 Scene X

The princess confides in Los her desire to escape an outdated, patriarchal society. Los convinces her to return with him to the renewed Russia.

Panel 11 Scene XI

Meanwhile, Gusev visits the Martian industrial slums, witnessing firsthand the exploitation of the alien working class.

Panel 12 Scene XII

Gusev addresses the crowd of Martian workers, inciting them to revolt. Some join, while others fear Tuskub's military force.

Panel 13 Scene XIII

Tuskub is informed of the Earthlings' mission; the army pursues them as civil war erupts with Martian workers embracing Bolshevik ideals.

Panel 14 Scene XIV

Los is separated from Aelita as the uprising collapses. The lovers share one last embrace as Tuskub's guards apprehend the princess.

Panel 15 Scene XV

The Earthlings reach their spacecraft and attempt to tune into the royal palace radio transmitter, where a desperate Aelita broadcasts signals across the cosmos.

Panel 16 Scene XVI

During the cold Russian winter nights, Los gazes at the cosmic sky, dreaming of the alien princess's phantom form, contemplating a new mission.
