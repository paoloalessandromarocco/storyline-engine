Shot 1 Medium shot — JOHN. Perspective depth of the surrounding space

Underground garage, night. John walks with a slow, deliberate pace down the centre of the garage corridor, between symmetrical rows of two-tone pillars — white on top, ochre yellow below. The floor is slightly wet. Cold fluorescent light falls from above: John's warm skin tone contrasts with the cold neon, while the dark brown leather of his jacket absorbs the light in a setting of dark grey concrete, white and ochre yellow.

Shot 2 Medium close-up — ELENA. In line with shot 1, centred in the garage

Underground garage, night. Elena, pale and motionless, gaze fixed straight ahead. Highly focused, tense — the heist is about to begin.

Shot 3 Close-up — JOHN. Asymmetrical reverse angle of shot 2

Underground garage, night. John, leaning against a rough concrete pillar, waits motionless with an intense, fixed gaze — deeply focused. Lighting identical to shot 1.

Shot 4 Close-up — JOHN in profile. Static camera at eye level

Underground garage, night. John stands in front of his black Ford Mustang, poorly parked behind him. He watches Elena out of frame and holds the car keys with a self-satisfied air. Cold fluorescent light from the ceiling; a raking beam from above grazes across the wet concrete floor.

Shot 5 Extreme close-up — objects (weapons detail)

Underground garage, night. Close insert: the Mustang's trunk is open, its grey foam lining cradling the arsenal — a magnum, a Beretta, two pump-action shotguns and boxes of ammunition. No character in frame. Fluorescent ceiling light falls vertically into the open trunk, creating sharp pinpoint reflections on the metal surfaces (the Magnum's chrome barrel, the Beretta's slide), while the black foam absorbs nearly all the light.

Shot 6 Two-shot / close-up — short focal length, blurred background

Underground garage, night. In front of the passenger door, Elena loads the Beretta with a quick, efficient motion and raises it to test the grip and balance, eyes half-closed in deep concentration. Beside her, John watches with equal focus. Palette: black, leather brown, concrete, neon light.

Shot 7 Medium shot — shallow depth of field, blurred background

Underground garage, night. In the foreground on the left, John looks intensely and warily toward the viewer; to his right, Elena is seen from behind as she gets into the passenger side of the car. Cold, desaturated tones dominate — blue-greens, concrete greys, the deep blacks of the car and leather jacket — under neon light that casts soft but deep shadows, heightening the noir atmosphere. Reflections on the car's glossy black bodywork carve out sharp highlights that define its shape.

Shot 8 Extreme wide shot — panning action shot

Underground garage, night. The Mustang executes a controlled drift to the right between the symmetrical rows of pillars, tyre smoke and vapour rising from the friction on the wet floor. Fluorescent light, no character visible — only the silhouettes of the two protagonists inside the car.

Shot 9 Extreme wide shot — similar to shot 8, slightly closer

Underground garage, night. Identical to shot 8, with the car framed slightly closer and the drift now swinging to the left.

Shot 10 Low-angle wide shot — interior/exterior

The Mustang speeds onto the garage's exit ramp; the city night is already glimpsed at the far end. Tyre smoke and vapour rise from the friction on the wet floor, headlights already on before the exit, no character visible — only the silhouettes of the two protagonists inside the car. The lower frame still holds the cold blue-green fluorescent residue of the ramp walls, while warm amber sodium vapour announces the exterior.

Shot 11 Low-angle wide shot — wide lens, full car in frame

Night exterior. A deserted American city. The Mustang, just out of the garage, speeds down a straight stretch of road. Reflections of streetlamps stretch across the damp air in warm light, over smooth, wet asphalt.
